Voice entry

Dictate your log by voice. Tap the mic, say what you did, and it's transcribed straight into the entry box for you to review. Your key is stored only on this device and used to call OpenAI directly from your phone.

Not connected Get a key →

Only the audio you record is sent to OpenAI, and only for transcription — no chat, no spoken replies. It uses gpt-4o-mini-transcribe (falling back to Whisper), costing a fraction of a cent per entry. Needs an internet connection and a secure (https) page.

Weekly targets

Set a weekly goal per bucket — a min to hit (e.g. Trading Biz) or a max to stay under (e.g. Scroll/YT). Progress shows in Insights. Leave blank to ignore.

Backup & iCloud

Save a copy to iCloud Drive so your journal is safe and reachable on any device. First time: choose your 30M Time Journal folder — iOS remembers it, and each backup updates the same file.

Never backed up yet.

On iPhone this opens the share sheet → Save to Files → iCloud Drive → 30M Time Journal. Browsers can't write to iCloud silently, so it's one tap per backup. Use Import to restore on another device. Your API key is never included.

Put it on your phone

Open this file over https (or add it to your Home Screen), then in Safari/Chrome tap Share → Add to Home Screen. It opens full-screen like an app. Voice needs a secure (https) page and microphone permission. Set a repeating 30-minute alarm in your Clock app as your nudge to log.
